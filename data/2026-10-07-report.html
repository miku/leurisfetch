Timeline

LEURIS lists publications from 1978 to 2026. Output peaks in 2021 with 5,334 publications, and every year since 2008 has more than a thousand.

Read the early part of the curve with care. The oldest record in the system was created on 3 February 2017, the day 5,201 publication records were entered: the initial import. Everything before is backfilled, and steps such as the jump from 2014 to 2015 (+3,438) more likely mark units starting to report than a sudden rise in output. 2026 is incomplete, as the snapshot dates from 7 October 2026.

Research projects follow a different curve. Starts peak in 2018 with 867 new projects and fall off since. Part of that is lag: projects that began after LEURIS went live were entered a median of 1.2 years after they started, so recent years will still fill up.

How Leipzig publishes

Leipzig publishes mostly in journals, and increasingly in English: the English share of publications with a known language grew from 64% in 2005 to 77% in 2025. Records name 31 languages in all.

Teams grow, too. The average publication had 3.4 authors in 2008 and 8.8 in 2025; the median moved from 2 to 5, so the rise is not only driven by a few mega-consortia.
